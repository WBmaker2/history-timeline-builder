# Surface brief: app (whole app redesign)

Scope: whole app (home, editor, compare, export modal). Visitor mode: Operate on editor/compare; Persuade on home.

Audience: 중·고등학생 constructing history timelines for class/assignment; teachers evaluate. Job: arrange event cards chronologically and draw causal relations, then compare to an answer key.

Chosen direction: Cork Pin-Board (IMPECCABLE'S PICK). World: cork ground, pinned index cards connected by colored string, pushpins, washi tape, handwritten labels. Memorable moment: a student's evidence wall where causal relations are colored strings between pinned cards.

Unresolved: dark/light (cork is light warm), mobile layout, print.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evidence wall, not a tool panel — events are pinned index cards on cork and causal relations are colored strings, refusing the neutral Tailwind dashboard every timeline app ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cork ground (#8a6a3f) with pinholes and warmth; index-card surfaces (#f4e6c8) with pushpins and slight rotation; colored string relations (#c94a2a, #2a6a8a, #e8a83a) with pushpin nodes; washi-tape labels; handwritten-feel titles over a printed meta line; warm shadow (3px 3px 0). Recognizable with all content removed.

STORY: The student sees their own evidence wall and feels ownership; they believe their arrangement is a real argument; they drag, pin, and string connections, then export or compare.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar as a cork strip with primary actions; left rail a stack of pinned index cards (palette); center canvas cork with pinned event cards and colored string arrows; right rail a notepad props panel. Primary action (+ 사건) sits top-right as a red pushpin button.

FORM: Cork Pin-Board, IMPECCABLE'S PICK (top-ranked grounded candidate), seed:99b8a88c44885957.
